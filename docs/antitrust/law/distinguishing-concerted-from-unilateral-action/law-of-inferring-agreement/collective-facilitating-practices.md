## Place in the Framework

Collective facilitating practices can support an inference of agreement when competitors jointly adopt mechanisms that make coordination easier.

## Core Questions

- Did competitors agree to exchange information, standardize terms, or adopt practices that stabilize coordination?
- Does the practice make prices, output, or strategic behavior easier to monitor?
- Are there procompetitive explanations for the collective practice?
