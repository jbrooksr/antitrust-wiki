## Place in the Framework

Unilateral facilitating practices may make coordination easier, but they raise the threshold question whether the conduct itself proves agreement or remains independent action.

## Core Questions

- Was the practice adopted independently or as part of a shared understanding?
- Does the practice invite, signal, monitor, or enforce coordinated behavior?
- When can unilateral conduct serve as a plus factor?
