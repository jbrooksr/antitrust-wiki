# Economics of collusion

**Problem of proving conspiracy:** most of the time, evidence is hidden, so P must often rely on circumstantial evidence. The economics of collusion helps construct a narrative that suggests conspiracy.
- Cartel problems and their solutions (see *Andreas* and *JTC Petroleum*):
    - Reach consensus on price / quantity / quality / markets: meetings and discussions
    - Deter cheating
        - Detection: share information
        - Punishment: violence (OUS) or making business more difficult for competitors (U.S.)
    - Prevent new competition from non-participants and entrants
