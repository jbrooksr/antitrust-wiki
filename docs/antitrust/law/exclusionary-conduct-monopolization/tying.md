## Place in the Framework

Tying links the sale of one product to another and may be analyzed as exclusionary conduct when it leverages power or forecloses rivals.

## Core Questions

- Are there separate tying and tied products?
- Does the defendant have power in the tying product?
- Does the tie foreclose competition or create exclusionary effects?
