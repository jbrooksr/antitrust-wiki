## Place in the Framework

The power-plus-conduct framework separates lawful monopoly power from unlawful monopolization by requiring both monopoly power and exclusionary conduct.

## Core Questions

- Does the defendant possess monopoly power in a relevant market?
- Was the challenged conduct exclusionary rather than competition on the merits?
- Is there a causal connection between the conduct and acquisition or maintenance of monopoly power?
