## Place in the Framework

Standing determines whether a plaintiff is an appropriate party to bring an antitrust claim.

## Core Questions

- Is the plaintiff a proper enforcer of the antitrust laws?
- Is the injury direct or derivative?
- Would recognizing standing create duplicative recovery or complex apportionment problems?
