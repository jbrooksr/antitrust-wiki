# Judgments & defenses

**Judgments:**

<u>Clayton Act Section 5(a):</u> a final judgment in a civil or criminal antitrust proceeding brought by the U.S. government against a defendant:

Is prima facie evidence against that defendant in an antitrust action brought by other parties

    - This provides private P’s an enormous advantage in followup suits to successful government challenges

This rule generally applies with respect to all matters to which estoppel would apply

    - This is distinct from claim/issue preclusion because it is a statutory right created for antitrust litigants
- **Defenses:** not covered, not examinable
