# Statute of limitations

**Statute of limitations:**

<u>Clayton Act Section 4:</u> statute of limitations in civil suits (tolls begin after injury stops)

- 4-year statute of limitations for private or public antitrust actions seeking damages
- Runs from the time the plaintiff suffers injury
- No explicit limitation for private actions seeking equitable relief, though courts often use 4 years as a benchmark
- No statute of limitations for government actions seeking equitable relief

<u>18 U.S.C. § 3282:</u> statute of limitation for criminal antitrust actions is 5 years

<u>Clayton Act Section 5:</u> statute of limitations for private suits are tolled while federal government antitrust suits are pending and for one year afterward
