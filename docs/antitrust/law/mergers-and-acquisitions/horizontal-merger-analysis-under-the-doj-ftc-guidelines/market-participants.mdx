# Market participants

**Market participants:** firms that currently earn revenue in a market or have committed to enter a market soon

Also includes “rapid entrants”: firms that would enter the market without incurring significant sunk costs in response to a price increase (SSNIP)

    - These entrants enter on the basis of the pre-entry price and don’t expect their entrance to affect the price
- <u>Excluded firms:</u> entrants

Most entrants enter in a timely and likely manner, but their entry is often insufficient

Entrants enter on the basis of their expectations for the post-entry price. They must decide whether entering will be profitable given their entry will likely lead to a decrease in price.

- **Market shares:** calculated for all market participants where possible

Typically calculated using historical evidence: Agency also considers predictable effects of ongoing/recent changes

Can be calculated using (a) revenues (typical), (b) unit sales, (c) capacity, (d) reserves (e.g., *General Dynamics*), or (e) use, whichever is most relevant
