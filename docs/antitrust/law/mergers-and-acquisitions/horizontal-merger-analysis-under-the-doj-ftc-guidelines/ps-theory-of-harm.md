# P's theory of harm

The plaintiff's theory of harm explains how the merger is likely to substantially lessen competition.

## Contents

- [Coordinated competitive effects](./ps-theory-of-harm/coordinated-competitive-effects.md)
- [Unilateral competitive effects](./ps-theory-of-harm/unilateral-competitive-effects.md)

## Place in the Framework

After market structure and concentration evidence, the plaintiff often articulates a mechanism of harm through coordinated effects, unilateral effects, or both.

## Working Notes

Use this page to collect theories that connect the transaction to likely price, output, quality, innovation, or choice harms.
