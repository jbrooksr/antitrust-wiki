## Place in the Framework

Efficiencies rebut or offset competitive harm when they are merger-specific, verifiable, and likely to benefit competition or consumers.

## Core Questions

- Are the claimed efficiencies merger-specific?
- Are they verifiable rather than speculative?
- Are they large and timely enough to offset likely harm?
