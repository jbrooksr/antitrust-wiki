# Single-brand restraints

Single-brand restraints govern competition among distributors or dealers handling the same brand.

## Contents

- [Vertical within-brand restraints](./single-brand-restraints/vertical-within-brand-restraints.md)
- [Exclusive distributorships](./single-brand-restraints/exclusive-distributorships.md)

## Place in the Framework

Single-brand restraints can reduce intrabrand competition while potentially increasing interbrand competition through better distribution incentives, service, promotion, or dealer investment.
