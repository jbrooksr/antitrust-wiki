## Place in the Framework

Vertical within-brand restraints limit how distributors compete with one another while selling the same manufacturer's brand.

## Core Questions

- Does the restraint reduce intrabrand competition?
- Does it promote interbrand competition through dealer services, quality control, or investment incentives?
- Should the restraint be treated under the rule of reason?
