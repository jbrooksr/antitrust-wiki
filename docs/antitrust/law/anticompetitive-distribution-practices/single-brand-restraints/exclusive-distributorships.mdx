## Place in the Framework

Exclusive distributorships allocate distribution rights to one distributor or dealer, potentially limiting intrabrand rivalry while supporting distribution investment.

## Core Questions

- What exclusivity does the arrangement create?
- Does the restraint primarily affect intrabrand or interbrand competition?
- Are there procompetitive justifications such as dealer investment, service quality, or brand promotion?
