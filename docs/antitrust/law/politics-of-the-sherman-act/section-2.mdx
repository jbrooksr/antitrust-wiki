# Section 2

**The continuum of political theories about Section 2:**

<u>Libertarian:</u> monopoly is legal as long as the monopolist doesn't do something that is otherwise illegal (punish with criminal/tort law, no need for the Sherman Act)

<u>Populist right:</u> unclear what they believe, writings of Josh Hawley are the best source

<u>Center right:</u> exclusion should be limited to conduct actuated solely by a desire to prevent competition (Judge Bork)

<u>Center left:</u> exclusion should be broader than conduct actuated solely by a desire to prevent competition (Judge Learned Hand)
- Conduct can be exclusionary even if it is motivated by a valid business justification

<u>No-fault monopolization:</u> the existence of monopoly power should be unlawful
- Broadest statement in U.S. discourse, even Khan didn't go that far

<u>Marxian left:</u> permit monopoly to facilitate proletarian organization and consolidation in opposition
