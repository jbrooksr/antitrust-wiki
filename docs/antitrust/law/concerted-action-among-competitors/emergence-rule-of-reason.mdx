# Emergence of the Rule of Reason

**Rule of Reason:** provides the interpretive framework for Sherman Act § 1
1. Initial burden: P has burden to make the prima facie case that there was (a) CCC, (b) ROT, and (c) antitrust injury.
2. If P satisfies initial burden: D has burden to provide some procompetitive justification for ROT
3. If D satisfies burden: P has burden to show that the anticompetitive effects outweigh the procompetitive justification

**Per se illegality:** agreements without a legally recognized procompetitive justification
- P must still satisfy its initial burden under the Rule of Reason (CCC, ROT, and antitrust injury) - an additional showing of market power is sometimes required
