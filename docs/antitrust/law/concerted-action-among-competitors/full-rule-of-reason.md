## Place in the Framework

The full rule of reason is the default framework for restraints that are not condemned per se and are not suitable for abbreviated review.

## Core Questions

- What is the relevant market?
- Does the restraint produce anticompetitive effects?
- What procompetitive justifications does the defendant offer?
- Are less restrictive alternatives available?
