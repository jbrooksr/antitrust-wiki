## Place in the Framework

Joint ventures can involve agreements among competitors, but integrated collaborations often receive rule of reason treatment rather than per se condemnation.

## Core Questions

- Is the restraint naked or ancillary to the venture?
- Does the venture create a new product, service, capacity, or efficiency?
- Which restraints are reasonably necessary to the collaboration?
