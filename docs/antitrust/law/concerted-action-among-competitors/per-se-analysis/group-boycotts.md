## Place in the Framework

Group boycotts involve coordinated refusals to deal, but modern doctrine applies per se treatment more cautiously than in price fixing or market division cases.

## Core Questions

- Is the boycott exclusionary, collusive, or both?
- Does the group have market power or control access to something competitively important?
- Are there plausible procompetitive justifications?
