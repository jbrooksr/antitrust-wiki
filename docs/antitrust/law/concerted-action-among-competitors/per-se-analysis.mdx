# Per Se Analysis

**Cases on the boundary of per se illegality:** overlap of potentially illegal agreements with otherwise legitimate business activities'
- *Topco* (grocery brand)
- *BRG* (non-compete in licensing contract)
- *NSPE* / *IN Dentists* / *CDA* (professional associations)
- *NCAA* (competitive league)
- *Dagher* (joint venture)

**Considerations on the boundary of per se illegality:**
- Is the restriction too broad relative to any lawful purpose? *Topco*, *NSPE*
- Would prohibition prevent equivalent market organization by low-cost, substitute means?
    - *Topco*: probably not, co-op can still operate without geographic restrictions and stores can achieve geographic spacing without cooperation
    - *BMI*: certainly in the short term, blanket license may be unavailable if per se illegal
- Is there direct or indirect evidence of anticompetitive harm or market power?
    - *NSPE* and *BRG*: evidence of price increases
    - *Topco*: evidence suggests that competitive harm was unlikely
