## Blogs and News

Short-form commentary and ongoing analysis for tracking antitrust developments.

- **FTC:**
  - [Competition Matters Blog](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/competition-matters)
  - [Press Releases](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases)
- [U.S. Department of Justice, Antitrust Division](https://www.justice.gov/atr/press-room-0)
- **Law360:** current domestic competition law
  - [Competition](https://www-law360-com.libproxy.berkeley.edu/competition)
  - [Mergers and Acquisitions](https://www-law360-com.libproxy.berkeley.edu/mergersacquisitions)
- [BIG by Matt Stoller](https://www.thebignewsletter.com): anti-monopoly commentary on current events
- [The Sling](https://www.thesling.org): critiques of neoliberalism through economic analysis and competition policy
- [Competition Policy International](https://www.pymnts.com/cpi): scholarly commentary on current issues in international competition law
- [American Antitrust Institute Legal Analysis](https://www.antitrustinstitute.org/work-products/type/legal-analysis/)
- [Law and Political Economy Blog (Antitrust)](https://lpeproject.org/topics/antitrust/): progressive antitrust scholarship
- [Kluwer Competition Law Blog](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/competition-blog/): current international competition law and policy
- [Truth on the Market](https://truthonthemarket.com): scholarly commentary on law and economics
- [Cleary Antitrust Watch](https://www.clearyantitrustwatch.com): defense-side commentary on international competition law
- **American Economic Liberties Project**
  - [Research and Writing](https://www.economicliberties.us/learn/our-work/)
  - [Press Releases](https://www.economicliberties.us/category/press-release/)
