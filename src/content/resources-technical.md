Technical resources are aimed at readers who want denser legal materials, empirical tools, policy documents, and research pipelines for antitrust analysis.

## Agency Guidance and Policy

Primary materials and policy documents for understanding how agencies frame enforcement analysis.

- [DOJ Antitrust Division Guidelines and Policy Statements](https://www.justice.gov/atr/guidelines-and-policy-statements-0)  
  U.S. antitrust guidelines, policy statements, and enforcement guidance.
- [2023 Merger Guidelines](https://www.justice.gov/atr/merger-guidelines)  
  Current DOJ and FTC framework for merger analysis.
- [FTC Merger Review](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/merger-review)  
  FTC materials on merger review, second requests, and merger enforcement.
- [European Commission Competition Policy](https://competition-policy.ec.europa.eu/index_en)  
  EU antitrust, merger, state aid, and digital markets materials.
- [OECD Competition](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/competition.html)  
  Roundtables, recommendations, and technical competition policy resources.

## Economic Methods

Sources for empirical industrial organization, market definition, merger simulation, and antitrust economics.

- [NBER Industrial Organization](https://www.nber.org/topics/industrial-organization)  
  Working papers on industrial organization and competition economics.
- [Journal of Industrial Economics](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14676451)  
  Academic work on industrial organization, competition, and market structure.
- [RAND Journal of Economics](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17562171)  
  Research on applied microeconomics, IO, regulation, and strategy.
- [Journal of Competition Law and Economics](https://academic.oup.com/jcle)  
  Scholarship at the intersection of competition law and economic analysis.

## Empirical Data

Datasets and data portals that can support market structure, concentration, pricing, and industry analysis.

- [U.S. Census Business Dynamics Statistics](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/bds.html)  
  Firm and establishment dynamics by industry, geography, and firm age.
- [U.S. Census County Business Patterns](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp.html)  
  Establishment counts, employment, and payroll by geography and industry.
- [Bureau of Labor Statistics Data](https://www.bls.gov/data/)  
  Labor market, price, productivity, and industry data.
- [BEA Industry Economic Accounts](https://www.bea.gov/data/industries)  
  Industry-level output, value added, and related economic accounts.
- [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar)  
  Company filings for firm strategy, risks, acquisitions, and market disclosures.

## Working Papers

Research feeds for newer antitrust scholarship and empirical work before it appears in journals.

- [SSRN Antitrust and Competition Law eJournal](https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/antitrust-competition-law/)  
  Legal scholarship and working papers on antitrust and competition law.
- [NBER Antitrust](https://www.nber.org/taxonomy/term/616)  
  NBER research tagged to antitrust topics.
- [Centre for Economic Policy Research](https://cepr.org/)  
  Economics working papers, including IO, competition, and policy research.
- [Tobin Center Competition Policy Initiative](https://tobin.yale.edu/research/competition-policy)  
  Research on competition policy, market power, and enforcement design.

## Treatises and Advanced Texts

Long-form references for doctrine, policy, and technical antitrust analysis.

- *Antitrust Law: An Analysis of Antitrust Principles and Their Application*, Areeda and Hovenkamp
- *Antitrust Law and Economics*, Keith Hylton
- *Competition Policy: Theory and Practice*, Massimo Motta
- *The Antitrust Paradigm: Restoring a Competitive Economy*, Jonathan Baker
- *The Oxford Handbook of International Antitrust Economics*, Roger D. Blair and D. Daniel Sokol
